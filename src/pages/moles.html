Principles of Til Vichar Shastra (Vedic Mole Astrology)

According to ancient texts including Maharishi Parashara's Samudrika treatises, *Brihat Samhita*, and *Agni Purana*:

  • Side Polarity: Right-side moles for men indicate solar (*Pingala*), auspicious and action-oriented results; left-side moles for women indicate lunar (*Ida*), receptive and fortunate fortunes.
  • Color Significance: Honey-brown or red moles (*Rakta Til*) are governed by Mars and Jupiter and signify high authority and unexpected inheritance. Jet-black moles (*Krishna Til*) are ruled by Saturn and Ketu, signifying hard work, discipline, and karmic debts.
  • Shape & Elevation: Raised, round, shiny moles are considered pure and bestow full blessings. Rough, irregular, or hair-covered moles require careful attention and spiritual balance.
