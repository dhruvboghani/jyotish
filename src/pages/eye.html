Kamala Netra (Lotus Eyes) — Spiritual Royalty

Associated with Vishnu and Lakshmi. Reflects noble integrity, pure aesthetic discernment, and high spiritual merit from past lives.

Principles of Netra Samudrika Shastra

In Vedic physiology, the right eye is illuminated by the **Sun (Surya)** and the left eye is illuminated by the **Moon (Chandra)**:

  • Sclera Purity: Clear white sclera denotes Sattvic harmony; yellowish tones reflect Pitta (Mars/Sun overload); bluish-white reflects high Vata/Kapha.
  • Pupil Radiance (*Tejas*): Sages regarded the spark in the pupil as the reflection of the Atman (Soul). A steady, blinking-controlled gaze signals mastery over the mind (*Dharana*).
