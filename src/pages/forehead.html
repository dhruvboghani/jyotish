The 7 Planetary Creases in Vedic Bhāla Shastra

In *Brihat Samhita* (Chapter 68, *Purusha Lakshana*) and *Agni Purana*, the forehead is known as the **Bhāla Patra** (the tablet of destiny):

  • Saturn Line (Top): Indicates philosophical patience, longevity, resilience against chronic hardships, and deep concentration.
  • Jupiter Line (2nd): Governs dharma, academic honors, public ethics, and spiritual blessings.
  • Mars Line (3rd): Grants executive drive, physical bravery, strategic thinking, and leadership.
  • Sun Line (Center): Represents royal favor (*Raja Yoga*), public illumination, self-respect, and vitality.
  • Venus Line (5th): Imparts aesthetic charisma, love for poetry, luxury, and magnetic charm.
  • Mercury Line (6th): Bestows commercial acumen, mathematical skill, wit, and linguistic fluency.
  • Moon Line (Bottom): Reflects intuition, deep emotional resonance, artistic perception, and wanderlust.
