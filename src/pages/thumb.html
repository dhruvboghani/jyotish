Principles of Nadi Angustha Shastra

In ancient Tamil palm-leaf traditions (*Nadi Granthas*) attributed to Sages Agastya, Bhrigu, and Kaushika:

  • Index of Karma: The thumb impression (*Angustha Rekha*) is considered the unique thumbprint of the soul's current incarnation.
  • 108 Sub-Categories: The 4 primary archetypes (Chakra, Shankha, Dhwaja, and Gadha) branch into 108 subtle ridge variations reflecting karmic debts (*Prarabdha Karma*) and destined soul blessings.
  • Male & Female Polarity: Men present the right thumb, connecting with solar Pingala prana; women present the left thumb, connecting with lunar Ida prana.
