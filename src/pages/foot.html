Principles of Pāda Samudrika Shastra

In *Brihat Samhita* (Chapter 67) and *Garuda Purana*, the feet represent the foundation of the human temple (*Pratishtha*):

  • Deva Pada (High Arch): Reflects spiritual refinement, light-footed mobility, and self-reliance.
  • Manushya Pada (Balanced Arch): Reflects balanced earth and water elements, granting material prosperity and strong family bonds.
  • Bhumi Pada (Flat Foot): Directly grounded with Mother Earth (*Bhumi Mata*), granting endurance, patience, and realistic execution.
